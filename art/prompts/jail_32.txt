A single 32x32 pixel game sprite of a small frontier jail — a lock-up, a gaolhouse. True low-resolution pixel art: enormous chunky square pixels, hard edges, no anti-aliasing, no blur, no gradients, no soft shading. Every pixel a deliberate flat block of solid colour. 16-bit era sprite work at extremely small size.

CRITICAL — EXTREME SIMPLICITY:
This must read clearly at 32 pixels wide. Reduce everything to bold, blocky, essential shapes. NO fine detail, NO thin lines, NO small texture, NO intricate linework. Individual logs or stones must NOT be picked out one by one — suggest the walls with at most two or three broad bands of slightly different tone. The whole building is a handful of large, chunky rectangular pixel masses. Crude and bold, not delicate.

COMPOSITION:
Perfectly square 1:1 image. The building is centred and fills almost the entire frame, edge to edge, leaving only a couple of pixels of margin. Seen straight on from ground level, flat front-facing elevation, like a classic top-down RPG building. Not isometric, not angled, not in perspective. Slightly wider than it is tall.

THE SHAPE:
A squat, heavy, single-storey blockhouse — lower and stouter than a dwelling, built to hold people rather than shelter them. Walls of heavy dark timber on a low stone footing. The roof is one SOLID unbroken flat slab of dark colour, a clean rectangle with a straight edge — no shingles, no speckling, no texture, no chimney. It should look grim and solid.

1. A BARRED WINDOW, and it must be the loudest thing in the sprite. A large square hole in the wall, wide and tall, set to one side of the door at head height, filled with solid near-black. Across it run THREE thick vertical bars of pale cold grey, each bar two full pixels wide with a clear black gap either side, running the whole height of the opening. At 32 pixels this window should be instantly readable as a cage. Make it bigger and bolder than looks sensible.
2. A heavy door, centred: a dark rectangle reaching the ground, with a single bold horizontal bar or brace across it and a small pale block for a lock or bolt.
Nothing else. No sign, no text, no lettering, no lettering of any kind, no people, no fence.

COLOUR — full brown, 5 shades only:
Strictly limited palette of five flat warm browns, the same palette as the log cabins so this building belongs beside them. Near-black espresso brown for the doorway void and outline. Dark walnut brown for shadowed areas and the roof underside. Rich mid chestnut brown for the main timber walls. Warm honey brown for the lit top of the roof. Pale tan for the top highlights and the log-end rings. No grey, no blue-grey, no slate, no sage, no cold tones of any kind. The building is whole, healthy and sturdy.

BACKGROUND:
Completely flat, uniform, solid single-colour background in a plain neutral grey, clearly different from every colour in the sprite so it can be keyed out. No scenery, no ground, no grass, no sky, no shadow, no trees, no border, no frame, no text.

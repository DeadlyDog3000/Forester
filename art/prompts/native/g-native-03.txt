A late 17th-century oil portrait on canvas, in the manner of John Verelst's 1710 portraits of Haudenosaunee leaders: half-length, three-quarter view, a dark brown background, soft warm light from the left, visible brushwork and fine craquelure, a dignified and respectful likeness of a real person. An Anishinaabe war leader in his thirties: long black hair with two braids wrapped in otter fur, red ochre paint across the eyes, a beaded shoulder bag strap across a bare chest, copper armbands, holding a bow. No text, no frame, no signature. Square.

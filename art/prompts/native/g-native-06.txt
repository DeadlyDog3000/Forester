A late 17th-century oil portrait on canvas, in the manner of John Verelst's 1710 portraits of Haudenosaunee leaders: half-length, three-quarter view, a dark brown background, soft warm light from the left, visible brushwork and fine craquelure, a dignified and respectful likeness of a real person. A Dakota war leader in his thirties: long hair with eagle feathers worn upright at the back, a painted buffalo-hide robe over his shoulders, porcupine-quill work on the robe, holding a stone-headed club. No text, no frame, no signature. Square.

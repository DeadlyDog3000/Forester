A late 17th-century oil portrait on canvas, in the manner of John Verelst's 1710 portraits of Haudenosaunee leaders: half-length, three-quarter view, a dark brown background, soft warm light from the left, visible brushwork and fine craquelure, a dignified and respectful likeness of a real person. A Cherokee war leader in his fifties: a cloth turban with a few feathers, silver ear ornaments, a deep-set gaze, a white linen trade shirt under a blue stroud mantle, a powder horn on a strap, holding a long trade musket upright. No text, no frame, no signature. Square.

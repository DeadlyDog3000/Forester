A late 17th-century oil portrait on canvas, in the manner of John Verelst's 1710 portraits of Haudenosaunee leaders: half-length, three-quarter view, a dark brown background, soft warm light from the left, visible brushwork and fine craquelure, a dignified and respectful likeness of a real person. A Choctaw town leader in his fifties: hair worn long, a cloth headwrap, silver gorget at the throat, a red trade-cloth coat, holding a long ceremonial pipe with feathers on the stem. No text, no frame, no signature. Square.

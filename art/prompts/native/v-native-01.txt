A late 17th-century oil portrait on canvas, in the manner of John Verelst's 1710 portraits of Haudenosaunee leaders: half-length, three-quarter view, a dark brown background, soft warm light from the left, visible brushwork and fine craquelure, a dignified and respectful likeness of a real person. A Haudenosaunee sachem, an elderly civil leader: grey hair, a deer-antler headdress band, a wampum belt of purple and white shell beads held in both hands, a dark blue blanket with a red border, a thoughtful expression. No text, no frame, no signature. Square.

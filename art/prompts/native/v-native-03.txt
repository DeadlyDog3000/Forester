A late 17th-century oil portrait on canvas, in the manner of John Verelst's 1710 portraits of Haudenosaunee leaders: half-length, three-quarter view, a dark brown background, soft warm light from the left, visible brushwork and fine craquelure, a dignified and respectful likeness of a real person. An Illinois civil leader in his forties: long hair tied back, a calumet pipe decorated with feathers held across his chest, a painted hide robe, quiet authority. No text, no frame, no signature.

A late 17th-century oil portrait on canvas, in the manner of John Verelst's 1710 portraits of Haudenosaunee leaders: half-length, three-quarter view, a dark brown background, soft warm light from the left, visible brushwork and fine craquelure, a dignified and respectful likeness of a real person. A Cree elder: long grey hair, a caribou-hide coat painted with red designs, a necklace of bear claws, holding a beaded pouch. No text, no frame, no signature. Square.

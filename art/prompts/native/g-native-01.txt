A late 17th-century oil portrait on canvas, in the manner of John Verelst's 1710 portraits of Haudenosaunee leaders: half-length, three-quarter view, a dark brown background, soft warm light from the left, visible brushwork and fine craquelure, a dignified and respectful likeness of a real person. A Haudenosaunee war leader in his forties: head shaved but for a scalp lock with a red-dyed deer-hair roach and one eagle feather, fine blue-black tattoo lines on his cheeks and brow, a red trade-stroud blanket over one shoulder, a silver brooch, holding a ball-headed wooden war club. No text, no frame, no signature. Square.

A sheet of twelve small trade-goods icons for a 17th-century strategy game, arranged in a perfectly even grid of 4 columns and 3 rows, each icon centred in its own cell with generous plain space around it and nothing touching between cells. Style: hand-painted gouache and watercolour over fine black ink linework, like the illustrations in a 1690s merchant's ledger or a period map cartouche; warm muted natural colours; each object drawn alone, three-quarter view, with a soft small shadow. Plain warm cream parchment background, completely uniform, no grid lines, no borders, no frames, no text, no labels, no numbers.
Row 1, left to right: a beaver pelt stretched on a round hoop; a conical white sugar loaf wrapped in blue paper; a tied bundle of dried brown tobacco leaves; a split salted cod fish.
Row 2, left to right: a small stack of three cut timber logs; three dark iron bars bound together; a golden sheaf of wheat tied with straw; a small oak wine cask with a red wine stain.
Row 3, left to right: a tied bale of white raw wool; a bolt of shining red silk; a folded stack of blue woollen broadcloth; three gleaming silver ingots.

A single 32x32 pixel game sprite of one OLD TALL spruce pine tree, drawn to stand beside the spruce in the reference image as another tree in the same wood.

True low-resolution pixel art: enormous chunky square pixels, hard edges, no anti-aliasing, no blur, no gradients. Every pixel a deliberate flat block of solid colour. Authentic 16-bit era sprite work at extremely small size.

CRITICAL — EXTREME SIMPLICITY:
Must read clearly at 32 pixels. Bold blocky shapes only, no fine needle detail, no thin branches.

Perfectly square 1:1 image. One single tree, centred, seen straight on from ground level. Flat head-on view, not isometric. It stands on the same ground line as the reference and its trunk foot is at the same height in the frame, so the two sprites can stand side by side in the same wood.

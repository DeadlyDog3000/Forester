A hand-painted marine plate of the 1690s, gouache and watercolour on warm cream laid paper, in the manner of period ship portraits by the Van de Veldes: a great three-decked ship of the line of sixty-four guns, a tall dark hull with three rows of open gunports and gilded carving at the stern, full square sails on three masts, flags and long pennants flying, seen broadside-on in three-quarter view under sail on a small painted patch of choppy grey-green sea, centred with space around her, crisp fine linework of hull, gunports and rigging, muted natural period colours, soft even light. Plain paper background, no text, no lettering, no border, no frame, no signature. Portrait format.

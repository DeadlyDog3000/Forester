A hand-painted marine plate of the 1690s, gouache and watercolour on warm cream laid paper, in the manner of period ship portraits by the Van de Veldes: a small single-masted naval sloop of war of about a dozen guns, a low black-and-ochre hull with a single row of gunports, a large gaff mainsail and jibs, a red ensign at her stern, seen broadside-on in three-quarter view under sail on a small painted patch of choppy grey-green sea, centred with space around her, crisp fine linework of hull, gunports and rigging, muted natural period colours, soft even light. Plain paper background, no text, no lettering, no border, no frame, no signature. Portrait format.

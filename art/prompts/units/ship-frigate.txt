A hand-painted marine plate of the 1690s, gouache and watercolour on warm cream laid paper, in the manner of period ship portraits by the Van de Veldes: a three-masted frigate of about thirty guns, a long low hull painted ochre and black with one continuous row of gunports, square sails set on all three masts, pennants flying, seen broadside-on in three-quarter view under sail on a small painted patch of choppy grey-green sea, centred with space around her, crisp fine linework of hull, gunports and rigging, muted natural period colours, soft even light. Plain paper background, no text, no lettering, no border, no frame, no signature. Portrait format.

HOW TO PLAY [close]

Everything is done by picking someone, then clicking the thing you want done.
GIVING ORDERS
Click a personPick them. Their panel opens on the right.
Then click a thingThat is the order. A spruce is felled, a stone broken, a stake raised, a ruin rebuilt, wild grass gathered, a crop brought in.
Click bare groundThey walk there.
Click them againLet them go.
Right-clickCancel — drops the selection, the build ghost and the road.
Click a buildingOpens it: what it does, and what it is doing.
SOLDIERS
Click another soldierAdds them to the band. Keep clicking to gather a company.
Click the groundThe whole band marches there in column, and holds that ground.
Drag across the groundWith two or more picked, drag to form a line of battle. Drag further for a longer line, and they will keep the formation. Mouse only — on a touchscreen a drag moves the view.
Click a raiderThe band goes for them.
ON A TOUCHSCREEN
Press and readPut a finger down and a plaque says what letting go will do. Lift to give that order, or slide away to think again — sliding moves the view instead and orders nothing.
DragMoves the view about.
PinchZoom in and out. Two fingers also carry the view.
HoldClears the selection, as a right-click does on a desktop.
The side buttons✕ clears, ⌂ returns to your first cabin, ⟳ turns a wall, ☰ pauses. Heal and Deposit are buttons on a picked civilian's panel; the Chronicle and the Roll are along the bottom.
KEYS
W A S D / arrowsMove the camera. Hold Shift to hurry.
Screen edgePush the pointer to the edge and the map scrolls. Can be turned off in Settings.
Mouse wheelZoom in and out.
HSend the picked civilian to a hospital bed.
GHand what they are carrying to the town store.
CThe Chronicle — everything that has happened here.
PThe Roll — every soul, what they are doing, how they are.
RTurn a wall while placing it.
EscClose what is open, or pause.
STAYING ALIVE
Wood firstEverything is built from it. Fell, then deposit — logs in a pack are no use to the town.
Roofs before richesThe homeless freeze in winter and pay no tax.
Keep the larder fullFarms and hunters feed them. The hungry do poor work and worse.
Fair taxesGreed breeds rebels. The GOVERNMENT panel holds the dial and the whole bill.
Wall the townRaiders come at night, and they come for the stores.
Hints appear at the cursor as you play. Turn them off in Settings if you would rather work it out yourself.

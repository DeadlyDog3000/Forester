# Forester

**Alpha** — a pixel-art game about family survival, by DeadlyDog Productions.

Hamburg, 1683: expelled from the city for "Malicious Affairs" — your father executed, the rest of the family barely escaping — your family fled into the woodlands, far, far away. You, being your father's son, must cooperate with your sister to recruit and rebuild what your family lost on that fateful day.

## Project layout

- `art/references/` — hand-drawn concept references
- `art/sprites/` — finished pixel-art sprites

## Status

Early alpha — art and design in progress.

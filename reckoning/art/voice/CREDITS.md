# The voices

Men crying out — in pain, dying, groaning, and the grunts of effort — cut from recordings their makers gave to everyone on Freesound under CC0 (public domain): no credit is owed, but it is given. Each cry was cut out of its recording, trimmed, levelled and encoded to Opus for the game; otherwise unaltered.

## The shouted orders

- **order_*.ogg** (with **orders.json**) — the words of command ("Make ready!", "Present!", "Fire!", "Form square!" and the rest), voiced for this game with ElevenLabs text-to-speech in three voices made for it (a sergeant, an officer, a corporal); DeadlyDog Productions' own. Trimmed, levelled and encoded to Opus for the game.

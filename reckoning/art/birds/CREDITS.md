# The woods' birds

- **blackbird_*, chaffinch_*, greattit_*, pigeon_*, cuckoo_*, crow_*.ogg** — a blackbird's song, a chaffinch's, a great tit's "tea-cher", a wood pigeon cooing, a cuckoo calling and a crow cawing; made for this game with ElevenLabs' sound generation, DeadlyDog Productions' own. Trimmed, levelled and encoded to Opus. js/ambience.js sings them among the trees once loaded (the crow's made caw till then; the songbirds not at all).
- **leaves.ogg** — a breeze in the leaves of a beech and oak wood, the bed under the birds; made the same way, its end folded into its beginning to go round.

- **creak_*.ogg** — an old trunk creaking in the wind; **crickets_*.ogg** — field crickets on a summer night (made to go round). Made for this game with ElevenLabs' sound generation, DeadlyDog Productions' own; levelled against the made ones they stand for and encoded to Opus.
- **tropic_*.ogg** — a tropical forest by day, its birds and insects (made to go round), for the Wide World's jungles: made for this game with ElevenLabs' sound generation, DeadlyDog Productions' own; set against the leaves' bed.

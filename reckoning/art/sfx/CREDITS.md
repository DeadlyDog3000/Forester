# The field's sounds

- **musket_*.ogg, pistol_*.ogg, cannon_*.ogg, nade_*.ogg, clang_*.ogg, war_*.ogg** (with **sfx.json**) — the musket and pistol shots, the cannon, a grenade bursting, blade on blade, and a man's war cry, made for this game with ElevenLabs' sound generation; DeadlyDog Productions' own. Trimmed, levelled to their peak (not squeezed, so a shot keeps its crack) and encoded to Opus. js/audio.js plays them once loaded, and its made sounds till then.

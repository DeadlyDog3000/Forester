# The field's sounds

- **musket_*.ogg, pistol_*.ogg, cannon_*.ogg, nade_*.ogg, clang_*.ogg, war_*.ogg** (with **sfx.json**) — musket, pistol and cannon shots (played for the far-off ones), a grenade bursting, blade on blade, and a man's war cry, made for this game with ElevenLabs' sound generation; DeadlyDog Productions' own. Trimmed, levelled to their peak and encoded to Opus. js/audio.js plays them once loaded, and its made sounds till then.
- **owl_*, woodpecker_*, frog_*, duck_*, thunderNear_*, thunderFar_*, bell_*, knock_*, doorOpen_*, doorShut_*.ogg** — the woods' and the pond's creatures, thunder close by and far off, a church bell, a knock at a door, and an old door opening and shutting; made for this game with ElevenLabs' sound generation, DeadlyDog Productions' own. Trimmed, levelled and encoded to Opus as above.
- **loop_rainLight, loop_rainHeavy, loop_rainRoof, loop_brook, loop_wind, loop_murmur, loop_roar.ogg** — light rain, a downpour, rain on a roof, a brook, wind, a town's crowd and a battle's roar, made for this game with ElevenLabs' sound generation (DeadlyDog Productions' own); each one's end folded into its beginning so it goes round without a seam, levelled, and encoded to Opus.
- **stGrass_*, stLeaves_*, stDirt_*, stStone_*, stWood_*, stSnow_*, stWater_*, stMarsh_*.ogg** — a man's boots on grass, dry leaves, a dirt road, cobbles, floorboards, snow, in shallow water and in mud: each made for this game with ElevenLabs' sound generation as a slow walk, and cut into its single steps (DeadlyDog Productions' own). Levelled against the made footfalls and encoded to Opus.
- **loop_harbour.ogg** — the harbour's water slapping at a stone quay and the boats' hulls; made for this game with ElevenLabs' sound generation (DeadlyDog Productions' own), its end folded into its beginning, levelled and encoded to Opus.
- **knife_*, stir_*, toss_*, plate_*.ogg, loop_sizzle.ogg, loop_bubble.ogg** — the kitchen's: a knife through meat onto the board, a spoon round an iron pot, the pan tossed, a plate set down, fat frying and a stew at the simmer (those two folded to go round); made for this game with ElevenLabs' sound generation, DeadlyDog Productions' own, levelled against the made ones and encoded to Opus.

- **twang_*, xbow_*.ogg** — a longbow's string let go, and a crossbow's steel bow; **bodyfall_*.ogg** — a man struck down, going down on the earth; **chew_*.ogg** — a bite of bread chewed; **fly_*.ogg** — a fly going by. Made for this game with ElevenLabs' sound generation, DeadlyDog Productions' own; trimmed, levelled against the made sounds they stand for (measured through the game's own bus) and encoded to Opus.
- **wolf_*.ogg, frostCrack_*.ogg** — a lone wolf howling far off on a winter night, and a frozen trunk splitting in the frost. Made for this game with ElevenLabs' sound generation, DeadlyDog Productions' own; set against the owl, and encoded to Opus.
- **fifeN_*, trumpetN_*, zurnaN_*.ogg** — held notes on a wooden fife, a baroque natural trumpet and a Turkish zurna, a low and a high one or three of each, their steady middles folded to go round; the bands' marches are played on them, each note on the take nearest it in pitch. Made for this game with ElevenLabs' sound generation, DeadlyDog Productions' own; levelled against the made instruments.
- **snare_*, bassDrum_*.ogg** — single strokes on a rope-tensioned field drum and a great bass drum, cut stroke by stroke from takes of separate strokes; the bands' drums play them. Made for this game with ElevenLabs' sound generation, DeadlyDog Productions' own; levelled against the made drums.
- **shout_*.ogg** — a city watchman shouting "Halt!" down a stone street: made for this game with ElevenLabs' sound generation, DeadlyDog Productions' own; set to a man's cry. (The drum roll is the recorded snare strokes, beaten one by one.)
- **heart_*.ogg** — a heart's lub-dub as through a stethoscope; **breathM_*, breathW_*.ogg** — a man and a woman out of breath, a whole breath in and out each; **parry_*.ogg** — two musket stocks knocked together in a fight; **loop_runCrowd.ogg** — a crowd of men running over grass (folded to go round). Made for this game with ElevenLabs' sound generation, DeadlyDog Productions' own; cut at their quiet gaps, levelled against the made sounds they stand for and encoded to Opus.
- **swordDraw_*, daggerDraw_*, musketUp_*, bowUp_*, haft_*, file_*, halt_*, shuffle_*, brush_*, flag_*, shoulder_*, leather_*.ogg** — the armoury's and the muster's: a sword and a dagger drawn, a musket and a bow taken up, a spear's haft knocked down, a file on a gun lock, a company halting, men's feet turning, a brush along a stock, colours shaken out, muskets brought to the shoulder, a leather grip worked. Made for this game with ElevenLabs' sound generation, DeadlyDog Productions' own; cut, levelled against the made sounds and encoded to Opus.
- **handbell_*.ogg** — a small brass hand bell rung once (the cook's, for a dish done to a turn). Made for this game with ElevenLabs' sound generation, DeadlyDog Productions' own; levelled against the made one and encoded to Opus.
- **bowCreak_*.ogg** — a yew bow and its string creaking under the strain as it's held drawn, cut from a take of separate creaks. Made for this game with ElevenLabs' sound generation, DeadlyDog Productions' own; levelled against the made creak and encoded to Opus. (A gun's loading is heard on the gun crew's recorded rammer, art/foley/gunRam_*.)
- **armSwish_*.ogg** — an arm swung fast through the air (a fist that misses), cut from a take of separate swishes. Made for this game with ElevenLabs' sound generation, DeadlyDog Productions' own; levelled against the made one and encoded to Opus.
- **warF_*.ogg** — a woman's wordless battle cry; see art/voice/CREDITS.md.

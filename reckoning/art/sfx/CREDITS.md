# The field's sounds

- **nade_*.ogg, clang_*.ogg, war_*.ogg** (with **sfx.json**) — a grenade bursting, blade on blade, and a man's war cry, made for this game with ElevenLabs' sound generation; DeadlyDog Productions' own. Trimmed, levelled to their peak and encoded to Opus. js/audio.js plays them once loaded, and its made sounds till then.

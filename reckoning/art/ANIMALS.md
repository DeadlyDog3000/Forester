# The animals

Realistic, sculpted, furred and rigged models their makers gave to everyone under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0
(https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Converted for the game by tools/blender/sfanimals.py and realanimals.py:
scaled to life, textures reduced, only the clips the game uses kept; the wolf's and the fox's walk and gallop were made
for the game on their own skeletons.

# The field's close sounds

- **whiz_*.ogg, thunk_*.ogg, swish_*.ogg, hoof_*.ogg, horseFall_*.ogg, blow_*.ogg** — a ball whizzing past, a ball or an arrow into earth or wood, a blade through the air, a hoofbeat on turf, a horse going down, a blow landing on a man; made for this game with ElevenLabs' sound generation; DeadlyDog Productions' own. Trimmed, levelled to their peak and encoded to Opus. js/foley.js plays them once loaded, and its made sounds till then (the blow, which has none, is silent till then).
- **chop_*, hammer_*, dig_*.ogg** — an axe into a standing tree, a hammer on a nail, a spade into earth: each cut stroke by stroke from a longer take; **crash_*, fellStart_*.ogg** — a tree starting to go, and hitting the ground; **coin_*, purse_*.ogg** — coins in the hand, and a purse emptied on a table; **splash_*, plop_*.ogg** — something in the water, and a stone dropped in it; **build_*.ogg** — timber knocked into place and pegged; **packIn_*, packOut_*.ogg** — into a satchel and out of it; **fire_*.ogg** — a fire crackling, its end folded into its beginning to go round. Made for this game with ElevenLabs' sound generation, DeadlyDog Productions' own. Levelled against the made ones they stand for (js/foley.js lifts each set back to that loudness) and encoded to Opus.
- **cock_*, frizzen_*, prime_*, powder_*, ball_*, ram_*, rodOut_*, rodIn_*.ogg** — a flintlock loaded: the hammer cocked, the frizzen shut over the pan, the priming and the charge poured, the ball dropped down the barrel, the rod drawn, rammed home and put back; **parchment_*, quill_*.ogg** — a sheet unfolded, and a quill on paper. Made for this game with ElevenLabs' sound generation, DeadlyDog Productions' own; levelled against the made ones as above.
- **march_*, trot_*, sail_*, guns_*, spades_*, surf_*, wind_*.ogg** — the campaign map's: a column of foot marching past, a troop of horse at the trot, a ship's sail let fall and filling, guns far off beyond the hills, a siege's spades, and the surf and the wind over the heights (those two folded to go round). Made for this game with ElevenLabs' sound generation, DeadlyDog Productions' own; levelled against the made ones as above.
- **pickup_*.ogg** — something taken up: leather and cloth moving in the hand; made the same way and levelled against the made one.
- **fixBayonet_*, unfixBayonet_*, quiver_*, drawBow_*, crank_*, latch_*, boltLay_*.ogg** — a plug bayonet fixed and pulled out, an arrow drawn from the quiver and the bow drawn, a crossbow wound, latched and a bolt laid in; **oarDip_*, oarDrip_*.ogg** — an oar going in and coming out; **ladder_*.ogg** — boots up a wooden ladder; **scrape_*.ogg** — a boat's keel grinding aground; **tack_*.ogg** — a horse's bit and stirrups. Made for this game with ElevenLabs' sound generation, DeadlyDog Productions' own; levelled against the made ones as above.
- **gulp_*.ogg, sizzle_*.ogg, wash_*.ogg** — a man drinking, meat on a hot pan over the fire, and the water along a boat's hull (made to go round). Made for this game with ElevenLabs' sound generation, DeadlyDog Productions' own; levelled against the made ones as above.
- **hullHit_*, shotSplash_*, sink_*.ogg, sea_*.ogg** — the battle at sea's: round shot smashing into an oak hull and plunging into the sea, a ship's timbers groaning as she goes down, and the sea and the wind in the rigging from a deck (made to go round). Made for this game with ElevenLabs' sound generation, DeadlyDog Productions' own; set against the recorded cannon and the tree's crash, and encoded to Opus.
- **wallHit_*, wallFall_*.ogg** — a siege's: round shot cracking into a stone wall, and a stretch of it coming down. Made for this game with ElevenLabs' sound generation, DeadlyDog Productions' own; set against the ball's thud into earth and the tree's crash, and encoded to Opus.
- **animalFall_*, ladderFall_*, rockBreak_*, logsDrop_*, timberFall_*.ogg** — a deer shot and falling, a scaling ladder thrown down, a rock split, logs dropped on the pile, a timber house coming down. Made for this game with ElevenLabs' sound generation, DeadlyDog Productions' own; levelled against the tree's crash they replace, and encoded to Opus.

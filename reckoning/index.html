Henning's old bow

How to shoot

  1. Hold right-click to draw the string back. Hold it until the bow is fully drawn.
  2. Aim with the crosshair. It closes in as you draw.
  3. Let go of the button to shoot.
  4. Don't hold full draw too long: your arms start to shake, and the arrow goes with them.
  5. Crouch with C and walk. Deer hear you running.
  6. Arrows stay where they land. Pull them out with F.
